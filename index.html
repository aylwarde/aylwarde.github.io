Edwina Aylward

I am a PhD student at University College London under the supervision of Vladimir Dokchitser.

I am interested in algebraic number theory and arithmetic geometry. More specifically, my research concerns elliptic and hyperelliptic curves, the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture, and parity conjectures.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at Trinity College Dublin and the Part III course at Trinity College, Cambridge.
