13–16 April 2026 · Cheltenham

Women in Number Theory and Geometry Spring Retreat (WINGs)

A four-day retreat bringing together female and gender minority early career researchers working in number theory and geometry. Co-organised with Natasha Diederen, Silvia Gangeri, and Júlia Martínez-Marín. Event website.

2024–2025 · London

London Junior Number Theory Seminar

A seminar for London-based PhD students in number theory to present their work and learn about the research of their peers. Co-organised with Calle Sönne and Albert Lopez-Bruch. Seminar archive.

3–4 March 2025 · Stonehouse

Elliptic Curves in the Cotswolds

A two-day research meeting on elliptic curves and related topics, with 20 participants. Co-organised with Holly Green.
